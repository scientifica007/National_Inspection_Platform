# Roadmap

- Status: **Living roadmap**
- Updated: 2026-09-21

The roadmap is progressive. Future stages are design directions, not permission to pre-build them.

## M0 — Foundation / Domain

**Status: COMPLETE**

Outputs:
- product vision.
- invariants.
- domain model.
- authority model.
- lifecycle semantics.
- modular architecture.
- controlled implementation model.
- Slice 01 specification.

## M1 — Vertical Slice 01: Solo Inspector Field Visit

**Status: READY TO START**

Controlled increments:

- S01-I01 — Project Skeleton + Identity/Authority Foundation.
- S01-I02 — Local Institutions.
- S01-I03 — Local Checklist + Versioning.
- S01-I04 — Draft Visit + Frozen Snapshot.
- S01-I05 — Findings + Recommendations.
- S01-I06 — Finalization + Immutability.
- S01-I07 — UX/Integration Hardening.
- Human Acceptance 01.
- Human correction cycles as required.

Exit:
A coherent, usable, tested individual inspection workflow.

## M2 — Correction History + Official Record Safety

Design/build:
- Amendment/Addendum.
- explicit correction reasons.
- immutable lineage.
- official-record production readiness controls.

Exit:
The platform may be considered for real official finalized records, subject to security/deployment review.

## M3 — Missions + Assignments

Design gate first.

Capabilities:
- open/voluntary missions.
- assigned/mandatory missions.
- eligibility.
- Admin/leadership assignment.
- claim/accept flows.
- output-only vs constrained missions.
- deadlines/status.

## M4 — Teams + Operational Organization

Design gate first.

Capabilities:
- flexible team construction.
- multiple simultaneous memberships.
- contextual/temporary leadership.
- TeamCycle for periodic/seasonal formations.
- geographic teams independent of members.
- team workspaces/consolidation.

## M5 — Geography + Organization History

Capabilities:
- Wilayas/Dairas/Communes as versioned data.
- historical geographic relations.
- administrative/financial/current-work geographic relationships.
- organizational units and historical relationships.

## M6 — Knowledge Governance

Capabilities:
- References.
- Indicators.
- Legal/regulatory sources.
- Checklists/guides.
- local → proposed → approved → published by scope.
- versioned knowledge.

## M7 — Reporting + Datasets

Capabilities:
- structured reports.
- team consolidated reporting.
- provenance-preserving aggregation.
- attached tables/datasets.
- exports such as PDF/XLSX/ODS/JSON where justified.

## M8 — Dashboards + Semantic Zoom

Capabilities:
- Inspector dashboard.
- team/leader dashboards.
- Minister/national overview.
- People / Geography / Institutions / Work / Time lenses.
- drill-down from aggregate to source facts.

## M9 — Integration / Operational Hardening

As justified:
- notifications.
- external integrations.
- object storage.
- API adapters.
- performance/scalability improvements.
- deployment automation.
- advanced observability.

## M10 — National Human Pilot

Only after preceding relevant gates.

Includes:
- real workflow validation.
- role/authority validation.
- data governance review.
- usability across devices.
- maintenance/operations validation.

## Roadmap rule

No future milestone may be implemented merely because it appears here.

Each milestone requires:
- design readiness.
- bounded scope.
- acceptance criteria.
- controlled increments.
- review/correction gates.
